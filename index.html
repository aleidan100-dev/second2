Basics

Three parties, one tracking link

You do not hold stock, ship anything or handle support. You publish content that helps someone decide, and the seller pays you for the customers you bring.

Merchant

The company that sells the product and sets the commission, the cookie length and the payout rules.

Affiliate

You. A blogger, video creator, newsletter writer or page owner who recommends products to an audience.

Network

A platform such as Awin, CJ or Impact that lists many merchants, tracks clicks and sales, and pays you in one place.

PAY PER SALE

A percentage of the order value. Common with stores and software.

PAY PER LEAD

A fixed amount when someone signs up, requests a quote or starts a trial.

RECURRING

A share of a subscription for as long as the customer stays. Common with software tools.

Programs

Where to apply

Start with one network and one or two brands that fit your audience. Each button opens the official sign-up page.

Commission rates, cookie windows and which countries are accepted change often and differ by program. Read each program's current terms before you apply, and never promote a product you would not recommend to a friend.

Earning from your own links: after you are approved, paste your personal tracking link for each program into the MY_LINKS list near the bottom of this page's code. The button for that program then sends visitors through your link.
